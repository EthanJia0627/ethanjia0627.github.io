I'm Yiyang Jia, a PhD student in Computer Science at University College London. I study how robots learn new skills without losing earlier ones, and how they recognise when to reuse them.

My current focus is humanoid robots. When a new request arrives while a robot is already moving, how should it adapt without being told where one task ends and the next begins?
